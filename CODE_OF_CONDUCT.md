# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a six-person team building something under time pressure. That is exactly the situation
in which people get short with each other. So:

- Critique the design, never the person. "This mapping loses the interface context" is useful.
  "You broke the parser again" is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before rewriting their work.
- If you are blocked by another track, say so immediately and loudly. Silent blocking is the
  single most expensive failure mode on a three-week build.
- Nobody is expected to be available at any particular hour. Nobody is expected to apologise for
  sleeping, attending class, or having a life.
- Disagreements about architecture get resolved by the track owner. Disagreements across the two
  frozen contracts — the IR schema and the rule format — get resolved by the whole team, because
  everything depends on them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or publishing anyone's
private information. Any of these ends someone's participation in the project.

## Reporting

Raise it with any team member you trust. It will be handled directly and without an audience.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, the issue tracker, team chat, and any venue where someone is
representing this project — including the hackathon floor.
